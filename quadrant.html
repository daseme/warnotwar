Are we not at war but think we are?

All the weeks, since you asked

    Four Things, for now.

    Where this comes from

    the Dean Street correspondence, 8 September 2026open

    The heuristic on the front page came out of a text thread. So did this page. On 8 September 2026, with BNO a week into its longest stretch above 55, the thread read:

    8:15 AM Not sure the Dean Street Heuristic can hold up under market rationality

    2:19 PM Are we not at war but think we are?

    2:23 PM That could be divided into quadrants: are / are not; think / think not. I was thinking at this stage it's we are and we think we are, a condition we rarely seem to meet. March through May arguable we were and thought we were but seemed to recalibrate and did not think we were even though we are. Now it seems to be aligning. If the reserves really are at the breaking point, then perhaps all this is leading to proof that oil exists. I guess that would be the strategic endgame. Once oil exists, everyone will act as if we're at war

    2:28 PM there's no fighting in here it's the nonexistent oil room!

    The page takes the two questions literally and answers each with a number the site already keeps. The q and a page holds the first answer given that day.

    The six names

    Adam's grid has four boxes: are or are not, think or think not. The heuristic has a third answer between peace and war, the perpetual war sweet spot. So two more names cover the weeks that land there: we think it is perpetual war.
