## Source conflicts worth remembering
1. Pre-war war-risk baseline ranges 0.001% to 1–3% depending on source; 0.25% (Marsh/Caixin) is the most repeated.
2. TD3C was notional in Mar–May (no fixtures) and Mercuria disputed it in Sep.
3. STS volumes differ by definition (Hormuz-origin crude vs all Gulf of Oman liquids vs VLCC-loaded).
4. Goldman's components ($7+4+4+3+2=20) do not match its stated rise ($3→$20).
5. East-West pipeline attack date: 10, 11 or 13 Sep, or "August" (Signal).
6. October $/bbl Gulf–Asia: $21 (Braemar, Gulf of Oman leg), $24 (Kpler), $33 (Poten), $34 (Signal, Ras Tanura), $35 (Citi), $36–37 (IEA): route, vessel and date differ.
