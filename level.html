What 1994 is

1994 is Rwanda. Of the 824,177 deaths UCDP counts that year, 772,463 were in Rwanda, almost all of them civilians killed deliberately in the genocide, in about a hundred days from April. UCDP's range for Rwanda that year is 347,614 to 1,612,663. It was one event in one country. The five-year average carries it from 1994 to 1998, so the plateau in the line over those years is a single event spread across five. The five years to 2025 contain no event of that size. On UCDP's best estimate each of them had more than 170,000 deaths, 2023 narrowly, and the heaviest wars changed: Ethiopia in 2021 and 2022, Ukraine from 2022, Gaza from 2023, Sudan from 2023, second only to Ukraine in 2025 and the site of most of that year's deliberate killings of civilians. That is what the average above the Rwanda years means, by a small margin and with the last two years still provisional: not a worse single year, but a level sustained for five.
