?What is this about?
The Strait of Hormuz is a narrow waterway between Iran and Oman. It is one of the world's most important oil corridors. When officials, journalists, and analysts talk about "oil flows through Hormuz," they often use different numbers that sound comparable but actually measure different things. This tool helps you read those numbers critically.

Glossary

mb/d
million barrels per day — a rate of flow.
consensus
the figure the tracking firms roughly agree on, sitting inside their wider range of individual answers.
AIS
the radio transponder system ships use to broadcast their position; "dark" ships have switched it off.
VLCC
the largest common class of tanker, carrying about 2 million barrels.
condensate
ultra-light oil produced with natural gas; counted in some totals and not others.
ship-to-ship transfer
moving cargo between tankers at sea, which can hide where oil came from.
bypass routes
pipelines that let Saudi (to Yanbu, Red Sea) and UAE (to Fujairah) oil leave without crossing the strait.
