How to read this ledger. These are reported statements, normalized by this project — descriptions are records, not verbatim quotes; each links to its source. Status tags are lifecycle states (on the record, deleted, disputed), never verdicts about truth. “Anonymous” means the reporter knows who spoke; the public does not. Nothing is ever overwritten: corrections and disputes are added as linked entries. Completeness is bounded: this is every statement located across the swept source sets — statements can occur in gaggles, deleted posts and unindexed remarks, and coverage varies by month. Independent tracker estimates appear on the verification panel, where they are compared with the directly comparable claims.
